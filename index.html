Craving for a cheesy pizza?

Fresh dough, stretchy mozzarella and a very hot oven. Our pizzas come out bubbling and golden, ready to share (or not).

About us

We started with one oven and a simple idea: make pizza the slow way. Our dough rests for two days, our sauce is cooked in small batches and every pizza is topped by hand.

It has been our family recipe ever since, and it still shapes everything we serve, from the first slice to the last.
